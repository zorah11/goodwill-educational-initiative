Who we are

Local knowledge. Long-term opportunity.

Goodwill Educational Initiative UG Ltd is a Ugandan nonprofit organisation working from the realities of hard-to-reach island and rural communities.

Our approach brings education, school health, livelihood skills and responsible financial support into one community-centred model. Goodwill Nursery and Primary School in Buwooya Sub-county is a partner institution and operational learning base.

Why schools matter

A school can be more than a classroom.

In island communities, the school is a trusted meeting point. Goodwill’s model builds around that local anchor—linking learning with wellbeing, family livelihoods and community participation.
