Proposed flagship programme

Goodwill Education Revolving Fund.

A planned non-profit financing programme intended to widen access to education and sustainable livelihoods.

Regulatory notice
The Goodwill Education Revolving Fund is being developed to operate in accordance with applicable Ugandan laws and regulatory requirements. Final eligibility, financing terms and application procedures will be published after approval.

01 / Intended support

Education financing

Proposed affordable support for eligible education costs.

Agricultural inputs

Proposed financing for approved productive inputs.

Fisheries inputs

Proposed responsible support for eligible fishing livelihoods.

Small livelihoods

Proposed financing for qualifying household enterprises.

Financial literacy

Planned learning on budgeting, borrowing and responsible repayment.

Enterprise support

Planned entrepreneurship and business-development guidance.

02 / Proposed process

  1. Expression of interest

    Submit basic, non-sensitive contact information.

  2. Eligibility review

    Criteria will be published after programme and regulatory approval.

  3. Responsible assessment

    Approved procedures will consider purpose and ability to repay.

  4. Decision and support

    Final terms will be explained before any agreement.

03 / Frequently asked questions

No availability is claimed on this sample site. Official launch information will be published after approval.

No rates are published because financing terms have not been confirmed.

Eligibility criteria are awaiting approval.
