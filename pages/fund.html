Education revolving fund

Goodwill Education Revolving Fund.

A nonprofit revolving model helping children remain in school while building a responsible path towards wider livelihood support.

The model

Support that returns to serve another household.

Goodwill currently uses the education component to pay school fees directly to participating schools rather than handing cash to parents.

Schools provide receipts and participating families repay monthly into the revolving fund. This approach is designed to keep the resource circulating while helping learners attend school.

Responsible growth

Nonprofit purpose

Designed around service, sustainability and community benefit.

Clear governance

Fund management and decisions must be accountable and documented.

Legal compliance

Licensing will be sought from the Uganda Microfinance Regulatory Authority where required.

Important: Wider agricultural-input, fisheries-input and small-livelihood financing remains subject to final programme rules and applicable Ugandan regulatory requirements. This website does not collect financial or identity information.

How education support works

  1. School fees are paid directly

    Goodwill pays the participating school and receives a receipt.

  2. Families repay monthly

    The school-fee amount is divided across the months so the fund can revolve.

  3. Another learner can be supported

    Returned funds help sustain education support and reduce absenteeism.
