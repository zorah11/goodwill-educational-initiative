In development

Goodwill Education Revolving Fund.

A proposed nonprofit revolving fund designed to make education and livelihood inputs more accessible to vulnerable communities.

The model

Support that returns to serve another household.

GERF is intended to combine affordable financing with financial literacy, entrepreneurship training and business-development support.

The proposed support areas are education, agricultural inputs, fisheries inputs and small livelihoods. Final eligibility, terms and application procedures will only be published after the required governance and regulatory steps.

Responsible development

Nonprofit purpose

Designed around service, sustainability and community benefit.

Clear governance

Fund management and decisions must be accountable and documented.

Legal compliance

Licensing will be sought from the Uganda Microfinance Regulatory Authority where required.

Important: The fund is under development. Loans are not advertised as currently available, and this website does not collect financial or identity information.

How it is intended to work

  1. Community-informed design

    Confirm needs, safeguards and a sustainable operating model.

  2. Regulatory readiness

    Complete applicable approvals, policies and governance requirements.

  3. Clear public guidance

    Publish approved eligibility, terms and application channels.
