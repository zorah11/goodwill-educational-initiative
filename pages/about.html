About GEI Uganda

Service rooted in community.

Goodwill Educational Initiative UG Ltd is a Ugandan company limited by guarantee, without share capital, established to operate on non-profit principles.

01 / Background

Building access across Buvuma.

The organisation’s memorandum establishes GEI Uganda to support education, responsible livelihood financing, vocational skills, health initiatives and community infrastructure in Buvuma District and Uganda.

Its registered office is situated in Uganda. The liability of its members is limited.

Editable mission

Expand access

To expand access to education, practical skills, responsible financial support and community services that enable vulnerable people to build sustainable livelihoods.

Editable vision

Opportunity to thrive

A Uganda where every learner and vulnerable household can access the knowledge, skills and opportunities needed to thrive.

Editable values

How we serve

Inclusion, accountability, service, sustainability, dignity and community participation.

The mission, vision and values above are draft language awaiting client approval; the legal structure and objectives below come from the memorandum.

03 / Memorandum objectives

  • 01

    Establish and operate the Goodwill Education Revolving Fund to provide affordable education, agricultural-input, fisheries-input and small livelihood loans to vulnerable communities in Buvuma District and Uganda.

  • 02

    Provide financial literacy, entrepreneurship training and business-development services to beneficiaries of the revolving fund.

  • 03

    Mobilise resources, grants, donations and partnerships for the fund’s sustainability in accordance with non-profit principles.

  • 04

    Operate the fund on a non-profit, service and sustainability basis, comply with applicable Tier 4 microfinance law, and seek UMRA licensing where required.

  • 05

    Provide vocational and skills training in construction, information technology, agriculture, tailoring and entrepreneurship with Goodwill Nursery and Primary School in Buwooya Sub-county.

  • 06

    Provide scholarships, mentorship and career guidance to disadvantaged students, with emphasis on girls and persons with disabilities.

  • 07

    Initiate the Buvuma Island Schools Health and HIV Support Initiative.

  • 08

    Collaborate with Government of Uganda ministries, local government, development partners and NGOs.

  • 09

    Undertake construction, rehabilitation and infrastructure development for education facilities, health centres, community learning hubs, water and sanitation systems, and related structures.

Partner institution

Goodwill Nursery and Primary School

The memorandum specifically identifies collaboration with the school in Buwooya Sub-county, Buvuma District for vocational and skills training.
