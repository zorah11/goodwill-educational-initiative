# Goodwill Educational Initiative Uganda

Multi-page static website for Goodwill Educational Initiative UG Ltd, ready for GitHub Pages.

## Verified source material

- Memorandum and Articles of Association supplied by the organisation
- Organisation executive summary supplied in September 2026
- Mayanja Charles professional profile
- Organisation-supplied photographs of Goodwill learners and school-community activities in Buvuma

## Editorial safeguards

- The Goodwill Education Revolving Fund is described as under development; the site does not claim that loans are currently available or that regulatory licensing is complete.
- No National IDs, signatures, witness details, private addresses or referees' contact details are published.
- No impact totals, donor claims, testimonials, scholarship dates, financing terms or application criteria are invented.
- Before future photo publication, the organisation should retain evidence of appropriate consent, particularly for photographs of children.
